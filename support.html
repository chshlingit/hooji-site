支援

最後更新：2026 年 7 月 26 日

直接聯絡：hooji.app@gmail.com
夥計是個人開發的 App，通常會在 2–3 個工作日內回覆。

回報問題時，附上這些會快很多

  • 裝置型號與系統版本（例如 iPhone 15 Pro、iOS 26.1）。
  • App 版本：App 內「設定 → 關於 → 版本」，長按可複製。
  • 你說（或打）的那一句話，以及你預期會記成什麼。
  • 如果畫面有錯誤訊息，附上截圖。

請不要在信件中提供密碼、信用卡號或 Apple 帳號憑證——我們不會、也不需要跟你要這些。

常見狀況排解

說了話卻沒有記成一筆帳

  • 確認說出來的內容包含金額，以及買了什麼或在哪買。夥計只在缺少這兩者時才會回問。
  • AI 記帳需要網路連線，離線時請改用手動新增。
  • 試用到期後 AI 記帳會關閉，此時只會看到升級提示——手動記帳仍可使用。

麥克風沒有反應

  • iPhone：設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 麥克風／語音辨識，確認夥計已開啟。
  • Apple Watch：手錶上的設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 麥克風。
  • 記得是押住金幣說話、放開才送出；太快放開會被視為沒說話。

Apple Watch 記不到帳

  • 手錶負責錄音，實際辨識與寫入在 iPhone 上完成——請確認 iPhone 在旁邊、藍牙開啟，且已安裝夥計。
  • 修改、刪除與查詢結果目前需要在 iPhone 上完成。
  • 手錶顯示需要升級時，請在 iPhone 上訂閱或確認試用狀態，狀態會自動推送到手錶。

Siri 沒有反應

  • 試著說完整一點：「用夥計記早餐 100 元」。
  • 第一次使用可能需要在 iPhone「設定 → Siri → App 捷徑」中確認夥計的捷徑已啟用。
  • Siri 記帳屬於 Plus 功能，免費層會提示升級。

換了裝置，資料沒出現

  • 兩台裝置必須登入同一個 iCloud 帳號，且「設定 → 你的名字 → iCloud」中已允許夥計使用 iCloud。
  • 首次同步需要網路，資料量大時可能要等幾分鐘。
  • 我們沒有你的資料副本，因此無法從伺服器替你還原——同步是唯一的搬家方式。

訂閱與付款

  • 管理或取消：App 內「設定 → 管理訂閱」，或 iPhone「設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱項目」。取消後可用到當期結束。
  • 恢復購買：換裝置或重裝後，到付費頁或「設定 → 恢復購買」按一下即可。
  • 退款：所有交易由 Apple 處理，請至 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請；我們無法代為退款。
  • 試用：7 天全功能、不必先綁付款、不會自動扣款；到期只是降級成免費層。

刪除你的資料

App 內「設定 → 刪除所有資料」，兩次確認後永久刪除這台裝置與 iCloud 上的全部支出，無法復原。因為夥計沒有帳號系統，也沒有需要另外刪除的伺服器資料。詳見隱私權政策。

Troubleshooting

I said something but nothing was logged

  • Make sure the sentence includes an amount and what you bought or where. Those are the only two things Hooji ever asks you to repeat.
  • AI logging needs an internet connection. Offline, add the expense manually.
  • After the trial ends AI logging is switched off and you will see an upgrade prompt instead — manual logging still works.

The microphone does not respond

  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition, and check Hooji is enabled.
  • Apple Watch: Settings on the watch → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  • Remember to hold the coin while speaking and release to send. Releasing too quickly counts as saying nothing.

Logging from Apple Watch does not work

  • The watch records, but recognition and writing happen on your iPhone — keep the iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on and Hooji installed.
  • Editing, deleting and viewing search results currently need to be done on the iPhone.
  • If the watch says an upgrade is required, subscribe or check the trial status on your iPhone; the entitlement is pushed to the watch automatically.

Siri does not respond

  • Try the fuller phrasing: “Log 100 for breakfast in Hooji.”
  • The first time, check the Hooji shortcut is enabled in iOS Settings → Siri → App Shortcuts.
  • Siri logging is a Plus feature; the free tier shows an upgrade prompt.

My data did not appear on a new device

  • Both devices must be signed in to the same iCloud account, with Hooji allowed to use iCloud under Settings → your name → iCloud.
  • The first sync needs a connection and can take a few minutes if you have a lot of expenses.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so we cannot restore it from a server — iCloud sync is the only way it moves.

Subscription and billing

  • Manage or cancel: in the app under Settings → Manage subscription, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep access until the period ends.
  • Restore purchases: after reinstalling or switching device, tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings.
  • Refunds: all transactions are handled by Apple — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.
  • Trial: 7 days of everything, no payment details, no automatic charge. When it ends you simply move to the free tier.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete all data. After two confirmations every expense on this device and in iCloud is permanently erased and cannot be recovered. Hooji has no account system, so there is no server-side data left to delete. See the Privacy Policy for details.
